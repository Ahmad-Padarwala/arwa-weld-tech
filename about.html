Who We Are

What began as a small vision in Gujarat in 2007 has grown into what is now Arwa Weld Technologies—one of India’s trusted OEM welding machine manufacturers. We are based in Banaskantha, Gujarat.

We also provide dependable welding solutions, specialised OEM support, and service that keeps your company operating efficiently.

What We Do

We specialise in high-quality import-substitute welding machines, including rugged transformer welders and energy-efficient inverter welders built for today’s industrial needs.

Our equipment is engineered for durability, consistent performance, and tough working conditions, delivering stable output, long service life, and dependable results—day after day.

Our Commitment

Across India, welders often struggle with unstable output, short machine life, and frequent downtime caused by unreliable imported machines.

Located in Banaskantha, Gujarat, we help our clients create stronger, more effective operations by supporting local talent development, training welders, and promoting sustainable manufacturing.

Infrastructure

Our manufacturing facility spans 7,500 sq. ft. and is designed to support efficient, high-quality production of welding machines.

From raw material processing to final quality checks, every step is carried out in-house under strict quality standards, ensuring consistent quality and faster delivery timelines.
