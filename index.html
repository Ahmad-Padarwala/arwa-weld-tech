Our Services

ARC SERIES

The ARC Series is built for welders who need strong, stable, and long-lasting performance. Powered by advanced IGBT technology, it offers precise arc control, fast hot start, and low spatter for cleaner, stronger welds. With high efficiency, long duty cycles, rugged build, and full electrode compatibility, the ARC Series is ideal for fabrication units, workshops, on-site repairs, and heavy industrial applications.

MIG SERIES

The MIG Series is engineered for users who need speed, precision, and consistent weld quality. Equipped with advanced inverter technology, it delivers smooth wire feeding, stable arc performance, and minimal spatter for clean, strong welds. With high efficiency, long duty cycles, a robust build, the MIG Series is perfect for fabrication units, workshops, automotive work, and heavy industrial applications.

TIG SERIES

The TIG Series is designed for welders who demand clean, accurate, and high-control welding. Powered by advanced inverter technology, this machine delivers a stable arc and smooth start for perfectly finished welds with minimal heat distortion. Its high efficiency and durable design guarantee long duty cycles across materials, making it indispensable for stainless steel work, pipelines, and all high-precision fabrication.

CUT SERIES

The Plasma Series is built for professionals who need accurate, fast, and distortion-free cutting. Powered by advanced inverter technology, it delivers stable cutting arcs, smooth starts, and minimal cutting residues for cleaner edges and reduced finishing work. With high efficiency, long duty cycles, a rugged build, and compatibility with various metals and thicknesses, the Plasma Series is ideal for fabrication units, workshops, automotive jobs, and heavy industrial cutting applications.
